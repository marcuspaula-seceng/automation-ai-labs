## TL-01
### Training colleagues  ·  🟪 MENTORING

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Colleagues did imaging, networking and stock tasks differently. | Repeatable work across the team. |

**What I built or changed:** Wrote operational documentation and trained colleagues on imaging, networking, stock control and IT-room processes.

**Tools:** Runbooks, templates  ·  **Metric:** not measured  ·  **Evidence:** none

**How to do it (teaching notes):**

1. Show once, then watch them do it.
2. Leave a one-page runbook.
3. Ask them to improve the runbook.

## TL-03
### Public step-by-step write-ups  ·  🟪 MENTORING

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Lab work was invisible to others. | Labs others can follow. |

**What I built or changed:** Published every lab as a step-by-step guide with screenshots so others can repeat it.

**Tools:** GitHub, Markdown  ·  **Metric:** 26 SOC rooms + 19 portfolio projects  ·  **Evidence:** Public repos

**How to do it (teaching notes):**

1. Write the steps as you do them.
2. Explain why, not just what.
3. Show the failure, not only the success.

## TL-04
### Continuous learning  ·  🟨 STUDY

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Gaps against current job requirements. | Honest status per tool; each plan tied to a lab. |

**What I built or changed:** ITIL quiz passed, Jira courses in progress, job-tools matrix with a certification plan (Jamf 100, FortiGate Operator, MD-102, SC-200), CISSP preparation, iOS forensics coursework, postgraduate study in Cyber Security (programme on hold).

**Tools:** ITIL, Jira, iLEAPP, Python  ·  **Metric:** ITIL 69% → 77% (pass 70%) · ~50 h Jira courses  ·  **Evidence:** Matrix, plan

**How to do it (teaching notes):**

1. List the tools the market asks for.
2. Mark each as proven, lab, documented or pending.
3. Build a lab before claiming a tool.
